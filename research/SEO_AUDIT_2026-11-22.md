## The 27 candidates with no mapping in this sample

Absence from a partial top-rows export is **not evidence of zero demand**. These candidates include food-specific entries, printable charts and supporting information pages. Some are useful to visitors even if they do not warrant standalone organic targeting.

The website currently does **not** generate a separate page for every keyword, and should never auto-index programmatic pages only because a URL or a numeric search query can be generated. For every candidate check:

1. Does the page answer an independent search intent rather than duplicate the homepage or another converter?
2. Is its ingredient/measurement reference correct, with a visible source and food state?
3. Does it provide differentiated function or information, not just a new number in a title?
4. Is there observed competitor keyword or page evidence? Missing sample evidence should stay explicitly marked *unknown*.
5. Should its permanent URL be included in the production sitemap, or should it remain a navigable noindex support page?

## SEO + generative engine readiness: verified implementation changes

The standalone **Grams-to-Cups** site now uses its already approved page and keyword set rather than creating new low-value URLs. Current status remains **81 working pages, 62 index-eligible, 19 intentionally noindex**. Actual production domain and Google indexing are unverified.

**Readable on-page answers:** The homepage contains a complete 100 g flour-versus-sugar answer with US cup units and the published ingredient weights. Each ingredient reference page now spells out both grams per US cup and US cups per 100 g, with the food state, measurement technique and original external source. The gram-number comparison pages and ten ingredient+weight pages already display exact source-backed quantities and also explain the rounding/food-state assumptions. All 11 standard unit conversion pages now have a full-sentence calculation example, distinguishing fixed-volume math from ingredient-based mass-to-volume estimates. Users and crawlers see these in the original static HTML without waiting for JavaScript.

**Structured meaning without SEO theater:** Only index-approved production pages receive JSON-LD. The site homepage emits a `WebSite` entity, all eligible pages have stable `WebPage` IDs pointing to that website, and interactive calculators also emit linked `WebApplication` entities. Breadcrumbs retain a consistent URL chain. Pages relying on published ingredient weights use a `citation` URL that is visibly linked in their body. Fake `Recipe`, `HowTo`, `Review`, `FAQPage`, `AggregateRating`, artificial dates and non-existent organizations are not emitted. Open Graph site/locale and X summary-card metadata are added without inventing an image.

**Quality gate:** `scripts/publication-audit.mjs` verifies the page/application/website IDs, canonical consistency, visible source citations, social metadata and absence of misleading rich-result markup. The independent `scripts/seo-geo-audit.mjs` checks 70 high-intent static pages and outputs `reports/seo-geo-visible-answers.csv`, which is kept private in GitHub Actions. A passed check only means the source, labels, direct answers and relevant context are present; it does **not** prove a ranking, an AI citation or an audience.
